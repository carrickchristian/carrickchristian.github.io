SS2027: Synthetic Spectra

A five-day learning workshop on synthetic spectra at the University of Wuppertal, organized together with Sven van Nigtevecht and Fei Ren. It is aimed mainly at master's students, PhD students and postdocs.

  • When15–19 March 2027
  • WhereUniversity of Wuppertal, Campus Grifflenberg
  • Lecture seriesDan Isaksen
    Irakli Patchkoria
  • DeadlinesSpeakers: 1 November 2026
    Audience: 1 March 2027
Programme

Monday is reserved for participant background talks. Dan Isaksen and Irakli Patchkoria each give a series of three lectures. Two further series are given by participants: one on Lin–Wang–Xu's solution of the last Kervaire invariant problem, and one on Burklund's multiplicative structures on Moore spectra. Supported by GRK 2240 and the Wuppertal topology group.
