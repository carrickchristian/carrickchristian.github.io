NSF Postdoctoral Fellow · Universität Bonn

Christian Carrick

I am a National Science Foundation (NSF) Postdoctoral Fellow at Universität Bonn, working with Markus Hausmann. Previously, I was a postdoc at Universiteit Utrecht with Lennart Meier and a graduate student at UCLA, where my advisor was Mike Hill.

My research is in equivariant, chromatic, synthetic, and motivic homotopy. I am particularly interested in (connective) higher real $K$-theories and fp spectra, as well as the various computations related to these theories (e.g. slice, homotopy fixed points, Adams, Adams–Novikov, etc. spectral sequences).
